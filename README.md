# The World in One Picture — Tuesday 24 November 2026

An entry for the make.fun bounty
[Sum up 24 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124).

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output directory, byte for byte.
`entry.png` (1024×1024) is a one-page broadsheet of that day's world news:
26 numbered stories across 7 regions — 10 illustrated cards plus 16 briefs —
with a UTC timeline, a day-in-numbers box whose every figure is backed by a
card, a pinned world map and a date-check box, drawn by `build_image_v3.py`
from the day data in `day_ni24.py`, cross-checked across Principia's daily
summary, Daily Summary Of, Horizon, Just Security's Early Edition, Broadcast
Prism and the IQMia worldwide summary:

- A US jury convicts Huawei of trade-secret conspiracy and bank fraud.
- Iran says four tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz (tagged CLAIM;
  a maritime centre calls the mine claims unconfirmed).
- Fourteen countries back the ICJ's Rohingya genocide ruling on Myanmar.
- Ukraine strikes Russian ships in the Gulf of Finland and off Libya.
- The COP31 climate summit ends without a deal after 50 hours of overtime.
- Gunmen hold 25 hostages at a supermarket in Cartagena, Colombia.
- The United States loses its measles elimination status.
- Senegal is restored as Africa Cup of Nations champion.
- Real Madrid rout PSV 5–1 in the Champions League.
- A drone hits Riyadh's PIF tower, wounding 11+.
